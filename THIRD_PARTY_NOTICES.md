# Third-party notices

The MIT license at the root of this release applies only to NeuroMetBench-authored material for which the author can grant that license. It does not relicense third-party material. Original upstream terms control.

Raw third-party molecular matrices are not redistributed in v2.1.1. The reproducibility package records source identities, access paths, and redistribution boundaries rather than copying source-governed molecular datasets into the release.

The embedded immutable v2.0.7 historical archive contains provenance records for three small third-party-origin fragments that were present in historical development packages associated with scCellFie, scMetabolism, and Compass. Those historical development ZIP payloads are not redistributed by v2.0.7 or v2.1.1. The historical archive retains their identities and license observations as provenance only.

- scCellFie historical source identity: upstream repository `earmingol/scCellFie`; historical version 0.6.2; MIT license observed at the recorded commit.
- scMetabolism historical source identity: historical fragment metadata reported GPL-3 while the upstream repository license observed during the historical audit was BSD-3-Clause. NeuroMetBench does not resolve that upstream discrepancy and does not redistribute the fragment.
- Compass historical source identity: historical version 1.0.0; BSD-3-Clause license observed. Compass was not executed in the external family because the required external solver license was unavailable.

Full source-specific provenance is retained in the embedded v2.0.7 archive and the v2.1.1 Online Resource 2 ledgers.
